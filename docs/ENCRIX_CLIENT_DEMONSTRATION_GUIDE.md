# ENCRIX Client Demonstration Guide

## Before the meeting

- Use a current browser and a stable network; open `/demo` and confirm the DEMO MODE banner.
- Do not sign in to an owner's or telecaller's live account in front of a prospect.
- Keep the feature matrix available. Explain that this is a synthetic interactive preview, not a client's live workspace.
- Do not use customer lists, real contact details, payment collection, real calls, or real emails during the demo.
- Confirm with the prospect whether they want a product walkthrough or a requirements discussion; record feedback separately from the app.

## 12-minute flow

1. **Context (1 min):** Explain lead access control goals and clarify that all displayed records are fictional.
2. **Dashboard (2 min):** Show sample totals, follow-up count, activity visualization and sample notifications. State that chart figures are illustrative.
3. **Lead workflow (4 min):** Search/filter, add a sample lead, edit its note, change status, assign it, record a sample interaction, then remove the test lead with confirmation.
4. **Follow-up (2 min):** Schedule a sample follow-up and complete it; clarify that it does not send a reminder.
5. **Team and timeline (2 min):** Show fictional team members and activity; explain that invitations are disabled in demo mode.
6. **Close (1 min):** Review live vs demo status, ask for the top three workflow requirements, and agree on the next safe validation step.

## Answering readiness questions

- **Does this demo save my data?** No. It uses in-memory fictional data and resets when you leave or refresh.
- **Can it call/email/pay?** No. Those actions are not invoked from this preview.
- **Can you import our records now?** Not into this shared preview. First confirm tenant setup, authorization, backup, field mapping and import validation.
- **Can we start a paid pilot today?** Do not promise activation until Production schema/entitlement reconciliation, payment readiness, customer terms and an approved onboarding path are complete.

## After the meeting

Record the prospect's workflow, roles, current data format, required integrations, security concerns, success metric, and next decision date using the support/feedback process chosen by ENCRIX. Do not put prospect personal information into source fixtures or this demo page.
